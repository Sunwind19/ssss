# The reason why I made this program
I felt the importance of conveying South Korea's traditional martial arts since the nation's influence is on the course of uprising today. Also, I have experienced the beauty of Taekwondo by learning it for several years. Those reasons motivated me to create the program on Taekwondo.

# What this is for
As I mentioned above, my program is for spreading the beauty of Taekwondo itself and its history to all across the world towards the people interested in truly understanding the conventional martial art of South Korea.

# What I learned
From creating my program, I could not only learn how people make effective programs, but also study about the topic (Taekwondo), by researching about it. Moreover, it was intriguing to collaborate with AI to build a program people desire.
